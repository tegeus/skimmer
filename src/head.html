MODE HOLD

The craft always swings round to face your pointer. In Hold to thrust mode, hold the left mouse button (or Space) to fire the engines, then let go and drift. Hold the right mouse button (or F) to fire the twin guns. A couple of hits will bring a palm down, and an acacia takes three. The desert has no edge: new ground is generated as you fly. An oasis sits just ahead of where you start. In Follow pointer mode the craft thrusts on its own, harder the further away the pointer is.
